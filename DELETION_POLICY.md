# SafePass Account Deletion Policy

To request the deletion of your account and all associated data from the SafePass app, please follow the steps below:

### How to request deletion:
1. Contact us via WhatsApp: [Click here to message support](https://wa.me/601120616323)

### What data will be deleted:
Upon receiving your request, we will permanently remove:
* Your user profile (name, email, unique ID)
* All quiz history and performance data
* Any saved preferences or workspace settings

**Processing time:** Your request will be processed, and all data will be purged from our servers within 30 days.
