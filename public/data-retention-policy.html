Data Retention Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

This Data Retention Policy describes how Driver 360 ("we", "our", or "us") manages, retains, and archives user data to comply with legal, regulatory, and insurance requirements.

1. Purpose of Data Retention

Driver 360 is committed to protecting your privacy while ensuring that our company and yours meet compliance, auditing, and insurance obligations. By using our application, users agree that we retain specific data to maintain a permanent safety audit trail, even after a user is removed from an active team.

2. What Data We Retain

When a user account is deactivated or removed from an active team, we securely archive the following information:

  • User profile information (such as name and designation)
  • Historical activity, logs, and application audit trails
  • Records necessary for insurance claims, safety compliance, and legal matters

3. Secure Archiving

Archived data is securely isolated. It will not be accessible in your daily management view or active operational dashboards. It remains in our secure databases strictly for legal, reporting, and safety audit purposes.

4. Data Deletion and Anonymization

We retain this safety and compliance data indefinitely as required by insurance and industry audit trails. Any data that falls outside of these legal, auditing, or insurance requirements will be securely deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Data Retention Policy or how your archived data is managed, please contact our support team or your organization's administrator.
